Sequence

  1. 00–02 sIdle: breathing, weight shift, blinks, eyes find the threat.
  2. 02–04 sReaction: head snaps round, brows drop, rear foot steps back, guard rises, body turns side-on.
  3. 04–06 sFighting stance: bent knees, weight rocking front and back, fists breathing.
  4. 06–08 sPreparation: weight onto the rear leg, punching arm draws back, hips and shoulders coil.
  5. 08–09 sPunch: hips and shoulders rotate, rear heel pivots, fist travels an arc, smear frames.
  6. 09–10 sImpact: hit-stop, two inverted impact frames, squash, shockwave.
  7. 10–12 sFollow-through, arm retracts, balance step, heavy breathing.
  8. 12–15 sTurns to camera in guard, slow head turn, smirk, breathing settles.

How it is animated

The front and side drawings from the reference sheet are cut into a jointed puppet: head, torso, upper arms, forearms, fists, thighs, shins and feet, each with a round cap at its joint so nothing gaps when it rotates.

Arms and legs are solved with two-bone inverse kinematics, so planted feet stay locked to the floor and the fist follows a curved path. Hair, the head and the arm smears read the pose history to trail behind the motion.
